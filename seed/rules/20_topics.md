# 20 · 题材母版（10 个，已去重）

> 每个母版给的是**该题材必须补的那几块**，不是全填。方括号换成你的内容即可。
> 素材里同题材的多份模板已合并为「母版 + 变体」，不重复保留。

---

## 1. 真实人像 · 自然光

**必补块**：1 核心元素、4 镜头、5 光线、6 皮肤质感
**要点**：皮肤不许只写 realistic，要写毛孔/细纹/日晒；表情要「不摆拍」；最后加「不美化、不重度修图」。

```
A photorealistic candid portrait of a [age] [gender] [occupation], [distinctive
feature], wearing [garment + fabric], [expression and what they are doing].
[Place + light source]. Realistic skin texture, visible pores, fine lines,
slight shine on the nose, a few stray hairs. [Lens + aperture], shallow depth
of field, natural color balance, [film stock] grain. Honest and unposed,
not beautified, no heavy retouching.
```

**变体**
- 海边：`sitting on a beach`、`thick green knit sweater`、`genuine joyful expression`、`85mm`
- 窗边老人：`elderly woman, silver hair, reading a book beside a window`、`warm natural window light`
- 逆光：`backlit by setting sun` + `natural skin texture`，只写「黄金时刻」不够
- 轮廓光：写落点 `rim light wrapping the hair`，别只写 `rim light`

**坑**
- 只写 `beautiful woman` + `85mm` 会得到塑料脸
- 单光源必须写死数量：`lit only by a single window`
- 手里拿东西必须写抓握关系：`holding a freshly glazed bowl`；出图后先查手指
- **纹理有天花板**：`weathered face` + 高龄劳动者已推满，再堆 `pore-level / sun damage` 会过冲
- 主体词会锁脸：`45-year-old fisherman` 连出多张常是同一张脸

## 2. 街头与纪实

```
Candid street photograph of [person + clothing], [action], on [specific
street type] in [city/weather]. [Ground surface state + reflections],
[background people and how blurred], [ambient light source]. [Lens],
eye-level, [framing]. Authentic fabric wrinkles, natural skin texture,
slight motion blur on passing figures, documentary photography.
```

**要点**：环境要与人发生关系（不是背景板）；雨/夜必须写具体光源；背景人物要说明虚化程度。

**变体**：雨天 `wet pavement reflecting city lights`；地铁 `fluorescent overhead light, slight motion blur`。

## 3. 环境肖像 · 老手艺人

```
Create a photorealistic candid photograph of an elderly [craft] on a small
fishing boat. Weathered skin with visible wrinkles, pores and sun texture.
He is calmly [action] while [secondary subject]. Shot like a 35mm film
photograph, medium close-up at eye level, 50mm lens. Soft coastal daylight,
shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, natural color balance. Honest and
unposed, worn materials, everyday detail, no heavy retouching.
```

**可替换**：`50mm` → `85mm f/1.4`；`soft coastal daylight` → `soft golden hour side light`。

## 4. 商业产品静物

**五件顺序**：产品 → **落地表面** → 光 → 角度 → 氛围。
漏掉「落地表面」和接触阴影，产品就像浮在空中——这是 AI 产品图三大假感之首（另两个：光太平、角度没人这么拍）。
光源写死数量和位置：`softbox front-left + rim light right, no fill`。

```
A premium commercial product photograph of [product + finish],
placed on [surface + its texture]. [Contact shadow behaviour].
[Studio lighting setup: softbox angle, negative fill], soft directional
highlights, [specific material detail], accurate reflections, [background].
[Lens], [angle], shallow depth of field, precise geometry,
professional advertising photography. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```

**变体与坑**
- 鞋类必写落地阴影 `crisp soft shadow`，没影子像浮空
- 玻璃/镜面靠侧光定方向 `dramatic side light`，不写反射乱成一片
- 透明产品三件事：瓶身材质 + 台面材质 + 倒影质量 `pristine reflections`
- 带刻度文字（腕表）：**先查表盘文字有没有乱码**，再谈质感
- 材质纹理词必须配侧光，平光下纹理词白写
- 不要用 `premium`、`luxury` 代替材质

## 5. 建筑与室内

```
Wide interior photograph of a [style] [room type], [3 named materials/furniture],
[tall/narrow window with curtain condition]. [Light direction and quality],
[ambient bounce]. [Wide lens + f-stop], [camera height], corrected verticals,
muted natural colours, architectural photography.
```

**先定光和时间，再写家具**
- 窗朝向决定冷暖：`west-facing` 傍晚蜜色，`north-facing` 正午平而冷
- 写清「几点」：只看「自然光」等于没写时间
- **人工光与自然光分开写**，混成一句模型常只留一种
- 道具只写一件点睛的，写多了会堆杂物
- 数量词更稳：`single arced floor lamp` 优于「落地灯」
- 外观写冷暖对比：`glass facade glowing warm at dusk` + `fresh snow`

## 7. 电影感 / 历史战争

```
[Shot type], [low/high] angle, [subject + iconic costume detail + weapon/prop],
[pose and gaze], in [era-specific setting] under [sky/weather].
[3-5 period props with wear], [crowd/formation in background, how far].
[Lens + T-stop], shallow depth of field, [subject placement], [aspect].
[Key light type] with [rim/kicker from which side]; [shadow behaviour on face].
[Palette: 3 hues + values]. [Skin/fabric/metal specifics + grime].
[Period-accurate production design]. Film-like grain, atmospheric haze,
restrained CG look. No text, no watermark, no modern objects.
```

**年代 cues 要成套**（缺一个就串到别的年代）

| 时期 | 必备 cues |
|---|---|
| 一战 | 羊毛哔叽大衣 + Brodie 钢盔 + 战壕泥泞铁丝网 + 早期双翼机 + 棕褐正色胶片颗粒 |
| 二战 | 相应制服制式 + 载具型号 + 战地医院/野战装备 |

**立场安全**：写死 `no insignia or badges of any nation`。
**跨镜头一致**：多机位长片把「同一个人、同一片场、同一间屋」写死在开头；一条提示词只讲一个画面。
**三层空间**：一半靠色彩科学 `desaturated color science`，一半靠前景/中景/背景三层交代。
**室内战争场景要写死光源** `lantern light`，否则模型补现代顶灯，年代感立刻破。

## 9. 图像编辑

**四段式**：改什么 → 改成什么 → 必须保持不变 → 哪些区域不受影响

```
Replace/Change only [target] with [new state].
Preserve exactly: [identity items — face, features, skin tone, body, pose,
expression, hairstyle, proportions / geometry, layout, camera angle, framing].
Match the new [lighting/shadows/colour temperature] to the existing subject;
keep realistic perspective and depth of field.
Do not alter anything else. Do not add text, logos, watermarks or accessories.
```

**专项**
- 保身份换装：逐项列出脸、五官、肤色、体型、姿势、表情、发型、比例
- 草图转写实：保留布局/比例/透视，补材质与光，写「不添加新元素或文字」
- 抠图：孤立主体、边缘干净无光晕、不加棋盘格/阴影
- 多参考图按编号分配角色：`image 1 is the scene, image 2 is the subject to insert`
- 迭代：一次只提一个变化，**每次重述要保留的约束**（「和之前一样」会丢约束）
- 避代词：写「短黑发的女人」而不是「她」

## 10. AI 漫剧 / 视频分镜

```
[Shot N · duration Ns] [Shot size] · [camera move] · [subject + one action verb] ·
[light] · [palette] · [environment continuity note]
```

**整段必带三样**
1. **一致性锚点（Lock Block）**：角色（年龄/发型/服装/伤妆）、场景（同一空间）、光线朝向、色板 —— 每镜照抄
2. **景别节奏**：远景交代 → 中景叙事 → 近景情绪，禁止连续三个同景别
3. **首尾衔接**：镜头 N 的景别/视线方向要和 N+1 剪得上（同侧原则）

**字段齐全才算合格**：`镜号 / 时长 / 景别 / 机位与运镜 / 画面内容 / 光线 / 色彩 / 一致性锚点 / 中文提示词 / 英文提示词`

运镜写法见 `85_camera_movement.md`。
